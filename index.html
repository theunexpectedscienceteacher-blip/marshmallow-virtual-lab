♨️ Conduction

Thermal energy transfers through direct contact between matter.

Think: a marshmallow touching hot metal.

💨 Convection

Thermal energy is transferred by the movement of a fluid (liquid or gas).

Think: hot air moving around a marshmallow.

☀️ Radiation

Thermal energy travels by electromagnetic waves. Direct contact is not required.

Think: energy traveling from a heat lamp.

🌡️ Temperature & Particles

As thermal energy is added, average kinetic energy increases and particles move faster.
