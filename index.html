🔥 Marshmallow Meltdown

Watch thermal energy move into a marshmallow by conduction, convection and radiation. Collect data, graph it, then use what you learned to toast the perfect s'more.

Your Mission

Thermal energy always moves from warmer objects to cooler objects. In this lab you will heat a marshmallow three different ways and see how the energy moves into it.

♨️ Conduction
Energy moves by direct contact. The marshmallow sits on a hot skillet.
💨 Convection
Energy is carried by a moving fluid (liquid or gas). Hot air circulates in an oven.
☀️ Radiation
Energy travels as electromagnetic waves, with no contact needed. The marshmallow is held near a campfire.

What you will do

  1. Heat Lab: Run one 2-minute controlled trial for each method. Watch the outside, the heat map, and the particles.
  2. Data & Graph: Compare your results on a temperature-vs-time graph.
  3. S'more Challenge: Pick a heat source, decide when to pull the marshmallow out, and build the best s'more.
  4. Analysis & Submit: Answer the questions, compile your work, and paste it into Schoology.
